                         FOUR OBJECTIVES AT A GLANCE
================================================================================

✅ OBJECTIVE 1: Retire Deployments While Preserving Claims
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What:
  Enable campaigns to transition through lifecycle states (Active →
  ReadOnlyMigration → RetiredReadOnly) while preserving all historical
  data and allowing users transparent migration to new deployments.

How:
  • CampaignLifecycle enum with 3 states
  • State transitions are one-way (cannot revert)
  • Read operations work in all states
  • Write operations blocked in ReadOnly/Retired states
  • Events emitted on transitions

Implementation:
  Location: lib.rs lines 286-304, 440-441, 796-817
  Types: CampaignLifecycle enum, 2 DataKey entries, 2 events
  Errors: 48 (CampaignNotActive), 49 (CampaignInMigration),
          50 (CampaignRetired)

Status: ✅ COMPLETE

✅ OBJECTIVE 2: Separate Pausing Without Fund Redirection
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What:
  Allow independent pausing of deposits, payouts, claims, and admin
  operations while preventing malicious admin operations from redirecting
  funds (critical security invariant).

How:
  • OperationPauseFlags struct with 4 independent boolean flags
  • Each flag can be toggled independently
  • INVARIANT: pause_payouts ⟹ pause_admin (if payouts paused, admin
    must also be paused to prevent fund redirection)
  • Errors distinguish each operation type for operator clarity

Implementation:
  Location: lib.rs lines 307-323, 445-446, 819-839
  Types: OperationPauseFlags struct, 1 DataKey entry, 2 events
  Errors: 51 (DepositsCurrentlyPaused), 52 (PayoutsCurrentlyPaused),
          53 (ClaimsCurrentlyPaused), 54 (AdminOperationsPaused)

Status: ✅ COMPLETE

✅ OBJECTIVE 3: Network & Deployment Identity Binding ⭐ FOUNDATION
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What:
  Bind campaigns cryptographically to their deployment and network,
  preventing redeployments from aliasing (claiming ownership of) historical
  campaigns. Prevents testnet↔mainnet and old_deploy↔new_deploy attacks.

How:
  • DeploymentIdentity struct: network_id (BytesN<32>), contract_address,
    deploy_timestamp
  • AssetNetworkBinding struct: asset ↔ network binding verification
  • Campaign stores immutable deployment_id and network_id
  • All lookups require deployment_id + network_id match
  • Cross-network asset usage rejected at validation

Implementation:
  Location: lib.rs lines 198-203, 254-282, 437-439, 743-777
  Types: DeploymentIdentity struct, AssetNetworkBinding struct,
         2 DataKey entries, 3 events
  Errors: 44 (DeploymentIdentityMissing), 45 (NetworkBindingMissing),
          46 (NetworkBindingMismatch), 47 (CrossNetworkAssetRejected)

Security Properties:
  • Same campaign_id + different network_id = different campaign
  • Testnet campaigns cannot be claimed on mainnet
  • Old deployment contracts cannot alias new deployment campaigns
  • Asset usage constrained by network boundary

Status: ✅ COMPLETE

✅ OBJECTIVE 4: Asset Risk Management & Review
───────────────────────────────────────────────
What:
  Establish a canonical registry of assets with review status, retirement
  capability, and issuer-risk tracking. Enable operators to manage token
  risk, warn users about suspicious tokens, and prevent use of EOL assets.

How:
  • AssetMetadata struct: token, issuer, verified_at, last_review
  • AssetReviewStatus enum: Pending → Approved / Flagged / Retired
  • AssetRiskFlags struct: reputation_risk, liquidity_risk,
    concentration_risk
  • Separate IssuerRiskRegistry for issuer profiles
  • Retired assets blocked from new campaigns

Implementation:
  Location: lib.rs lines 214-224, 326-375, 842-874
  Types: AssetMetadata struct, AssetReviewStatus enum, AssetRiskFlags
         struct, 4 DataKey entries, 4 events
  Errors: 55 (AssetMetadataNotFound), 56 (AssetNotApproved),
          57 (AssetRetired), 58 (IssuerRiskTooHigh)
